# Transparency Model

The source stays private; material operational facts should still be inspectable by authorized stakeholders.

## Transparent surfaces

- Architecture and trust boundaries.
- Evidence/package schema.
- Release/change history.
- Known limitations and unverified areas.
- Host requirements and approval states.
- Security reporting and incident process.
- Sponsorship use categories.
- Operational status and material incidents.

## Private surfaces

- Production credentials and secrets.
- SSO client secrets and browser-session bridge secrets.
- Cloud, SMTP, Telegram and signing credentials.
- Private CA material.
- Personal identity evidence and customer data.
- Infrastructure details that are not necessary for a stakeholder's role.
